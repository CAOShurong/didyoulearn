# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

DidYouLearn welcomes learners, educators, researchers, and engineers. Participation must be
respectful, evidence-seeking, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of
expertise.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, publishing private learner
data, manipulating study results, impersonating model providers, or presenting unverified
submissions as verified evidence.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `shurongcao0819@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect
the community or the integrity of a study.
