# Core study protocol

## Unit of evidence

One trial is one learner studying one task with one tutor condition. The tutor condition includes
the product, model label displayed by the product, mode, date, and study instructions.

## Recommended sequence

1. Obtain consent and create a pseudonymous participant identifier.
2. Collect background variables required by the pre-registered protocol.
3. Administer one randomly selected pretest form without AI assistance.
4. Assign a tutor condition within a predeclared stratum.
5. Run a time-bounded teaching interaction without exposing held-out assessment items.
6. Record critical factual errors before identity reveal.
7. Administer an equivalent immediate post-test and a novel transfer test without AI assistance.
8. Collect perceived understanding and cognitive-effort ratings.
9. Administer a delayed retention form after the declared interval.
10. Freeze the record, compute its receipt, and release only the fields allowed by the consent form.

## Primary outcome

For randomized studies, the primary comparison is covariance-adjusted post-test performance with
pretest score as a baseline covariate. Raw gain remains visible for interpretation, but it is not
the sole inferential statistic.

## Carry-over

Learning persists. A participant must not receive two tutor conditions for the same concept.
Repeated-measures designs must use distinct, matched topics and account for participant and task in
the analysis. Treating the same concept twice as a conventional crossover design is invalid because
the first teaching condition changes the learner.

## Identity and evidence tiers

- `community-submitted`: participant-selected product; identity is a claim.
- `evidence-complete`: required transcript, timestamps, settings, and hashes are present.
- `coordinator-verified`: the study coordinator controlled assignment and capture.
- `provider-verified`: reserved for evidence confirmed by a model provider.

Evidence tier and learning outcome are always reported separately.
