# Ethics and privacy

DidYouLearn is research infrastructure, not ethics approval.

## Human participants

If a study is intended to produce generalizable research, consult the relevant institutional review
process before recruitment. Consent should describe what is collected, why, how long it is retained,
who can access it, how withdrawal works, and whether de-identified data will be published.

## Data minimization

- Do not collect names, email addresses, student identifiers, or full provider account screenshots
  unless the approved protocol requires them.
- Use pseudonymous participant identifiers.
- Keep transcript release separate from score release.
- Remove free-text fields from public data by default.
- Publish aggregate cells only when re-identification risk is acceptably low.

## Learner safety

The sample registry excludes medical, legal, mental-health, and other high-stakes instruction. A
task's correctness review is not permission to replace qualified teaching or professional advice.

## Fairness

Prior knowledge, language, disability, cultural context, and access to paid product features can
change learning outcomes. Studies should pre-register relevant subgroup analyses and avoid
interpreting small subgroup differences as stable model traits.
