# Roadmap

## 0.1 — Reproducible local laboratory

- Versioned task packs and run records.
- Deterministic assignment and evidence receipts.
- Outcome scorecards with uncertainty.
- Browser-local study flow and fictional demonstration.

## 0.2 — Verified community studies

- Task-review workflow and public registry.
- Equivalent-form audit tools.
- Delayed-test scheduling adapters.
- Signed study manifests and dataset releases.
- Optional hosted coordinator with explicit consent and deletion controls.

## 0.3 — Cross-product blind arena

- Provider-neutral runner interface.
- Sponsored or institution-operated API execution.
- Model-version provenance and drift tracking.
- Pre-registered public study protocols.

## Research milestones

- Validate the measurement model with real learners.
- Estimate task reliability and equivalent-form difficulty.
- Publish comparisons only after minimum sample and evidence gates are met.
- Seek educator and learning-science review before claiming external validity.
